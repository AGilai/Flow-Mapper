Frame 1. New environment from any environment

#/envs/new, opened from "New environment" under the environment picker. Creates sase-hld from prod with three subsystems and six platform components. Duplicating a design is the same page with a design environment as the source and Full copy.

  1. The picker says what each environment is. Under the name: "harvested" or "design · from prod, Oct 21". A design environment can be deleted; prod can't (it has no delete action at all).
  2. Source first. Pick any environment; its components fill the table below. Three ways to start now: Blank, Components (objects only), Full copy (flows too, which is how you duplicate an HLD). Linked is disabled with "later".
  3. Subsystems are defined here, in left-to-right order. Levels show "Subsystem" now and "Domain" as a later level, so the path field (platform/identity) is understood from day one.
  4. You pick components, the page suggests neighbours, one hop. A neighbour is a prod component that calls or is called by a picked one. Each suggestion says why ("calls users-svc"), and stays unticked until you tick it.
  5. Each row says what comes along: endpoints, queues and topics, data stores, DTOs. Functions and flows never come along, and the summary says so in one sentence.
