Frame 4. Editing a flow in a draft

#/flows?env=sase-hld&draft=use-token-grant&tabs=sase-agent/tunnel.open. The flow "Agent connects to POP" in Edit mode, inside the draft that came from the discussion in rev 1: replace the new POST /token/exchange with prod's POST /token.

  1. View · Edit is a toggle on every page of a design environment. Edit opens a draft (a new one, or one picked from a list); nothing you change touches the environment until Apply.
  2. The draft bar names the draft, counts its changes (+1 −2 ~1), says who has worked on it and how many discussions it has, and holds Review changes · Discard · Apply. Anyone can open the same draft; there is no ownership yet (see the spec: a later invite model fits on top).
  3. Draft changes are drawn in place: what the draft removes is faded and struck through with "removed in draft"; what it adds carries an "in draft" chip. Review changes lists the same set as text.
  4. Full create, edit and delete on the canvas. "+" in a band adds a component to that subsystem. Dragging the "+" under a bar to another lifeline adds a call. Clicking an arrow or head opens its form in the panel. Delete is in the form, not on hover.
  5. Discussions on anything: the bubble with a count sits on what it's about: the flow (on the tab), a component (on its head), a call (on its arrow). The rev 1 thread now lives under this draft's change.
  6. The spark marks the highest new thing: heads of new components, and the new endpoint on a prod component. The calls of a new component don't repeat it.
