Frame 20. The flow's bottom pane: Notes, Comments and Action items, with step-range comments (new)

Maintainer (2026-10-22): the bottom pane under the diagram stacks two collapsible sections, Action items (frame 15) and Comments (frame 13). Make them tabs on one row and add a first tab, Notes: one long-form write-up per flow, like a Confluence page or a Word section (explanation, rationale, background). There is no separate “discussion” type: points under discussion are Comments and Action items. A comment can be anchored to a range of steps, drawn as a thin bracket in the diagram's left gutter.

  1. One tab row: Notes · Comments (6) · Action items (3 open · 5). Comment count includes replies (frame 13); action items show open and total (frame 15); Notes has no count (it is one document; the page shows who edited it last). One body is open at a time; the collapsible sections go away.
  2. Remembered twice: URL pane=notes|comments|actions and per viewer per flow (try/catch localStorage); the URL wins, so a shared link lands on the sender's tab. With nothing remembered the pane opens on Notes if the flow has notes, else Comments. Deep targets: item=4, c=12, edit=1, history=6.
  3. Notes is ONE rich document per flow. It opens read-only; Edit (the button at the end of the tab row) switches to writing. It shows “Edited by Dana Levi · 2 h ago”, a version number and History. At 1280 px and wider an outline of the H2/H3 headings sits on the right (sticky, current section in accent); below that it is hidden. Maximize (frame 16) is the best way to read it (20a, second window).
  4. Context-aware button at the right end of the row: on Notes it reads ✎ Edit; on Comments “+ Add comment”; on Action items “+ Add action item”. The ▾ always offers Comment and Action item and adds them without switching tab (a small form opens under the button; a toast “Comment added · View” links to it). “Make action item” on a comment stays.
  5. Cross-links switch tabs and outline the target (2 px focus ring, fades after 2 s): the comment's “created action #4” opens Action items with #4 outlined; the item's “from Dana's comment” opens Comments with that comment outlined. A “step 4” chip in Notes jumps to the step on the diagram.
  6. A bracket, never a box. “Comment on steps 4–9” anchors a comment to a range. The diagram gets only a thin bracket in the left gutter with a small speech-bubble count. Hover or click it: the steps take the light-yellow band (the 0.32.5 current-step token, no border) and the Comments tab opens with that comment outlined. The comment's pill reads “steps 4–9” and re-highlights on hover. Resolved comments hide their bracket. The layer toggle “Comment brackets” lives in Step details (on in design environments, off in harvested).

Purpose

Turns a basket into a confirmed order: stock is held, payment authorized, and one OrderCreated event published for fulfilment.

How it works

The storefront calls the gateway (step 1), which creates the order in orders-svc (step 2). Every line is then reserved in inventory:

  1. check stock levels (step 3);
  2. reserve each line, retrying up to 3 times (steps 4–9), with the out-of-stock branch for short lines;
  3. authorize the payment (step 8) and publish the event.

Rules and open questions

Rules for brackets (from the density check)

  1. Gutter, left to right: 4 px mark band (New / Changed, #75), 5 px gap, brackets in 12 px columns, bubbles straddling their bracket. Marks and brackets never share a column.
  2. At most 3 columns (2 under 760 px); further ones become “+n”. Columns are assigned by start step, longer first; a bracket reuses a column once the previous one in it has ended.
  3. Bubbles never overlap: placed mid-range; if two would touch, the later slides down 17 px inside its range.
  4. The band sits behind all drawing (below frames, lifelines, notes, arrows) and is the union of the current-step highlight and the hovered range; it never stacks darker. It spans the full width including the gutter.
  5. A range may start or end inside a loop or alt frame; the bracket does not move to the frame edge.
  6. Resolved comments hide their bracket; a replied-to resolved thread reopens on reply. If steps are deleted the range clamps to what remains and the pill reads “steps 4–7 (was 4–9)”; if none remain it becomes a plain comment.
  7. Layer off (harvested default): no gutter, no brackets, no gesture; the pills in Comments still highlight on click. Maximize keeps the gutter.
  8. Colour: brackets are the accent; yellow is only ever the band (the New spark stays the only yellow icon).

Rules for Notes

  1. Storage: the same sanitized HTML subset as comments plus h2 h3 and a step-chip element <span data-step="4"> (ranges: data-step="4-9"); mentions as in frame 18. Every Save is a version; History keeps all, shows the latest 30.
  2. Step chips follow the step number; a deleted step renders “step 4 (gone)” as plain dashed text, like the comment pill in frame 13.
  3. Concurrent edits: Save checks the version it started from; on a mismatch it offers “Keep mine (new version)” or “Open theirs”, never a silent overwrite.
  4. Every environment has Notes; harvested ones are editable too (notes are not harvested data). A full environment copy copies notes; Blank and Components copies do not.
  5. MCP: get_notes and set_notes (author, HTML or plain text, base_version) so an assistant can read and draft the story.

Mermaid round trip

  • A range comment exports as a rect around those messages plus a Note with the comment's first line; plain comments and Notes are not exported. On import, a rect holding a Note over becomes a range comment.

end

Open questions

  1. Do comments with a single step pill become one-step ranges? Suggest no: a pill stays a pin on one step with no bracket; only “Comment on steps N–M” (two or more steps) draws one. Alternative: bracket every pinned comment (more marks, one concept).
  2. Default tab: Notes when it has content, else Comments (drawn in rule 2). Or always Comments, as today?
  3. Resolve: anyone can resolve any comment (no accounts, frame 13), or only the author and the mentioned (frame 18 users)? Should plain comments without a range also be resolvable? Suggest yes, same tag.
  4. Should Notes be allowed in harvested environments? Drawn as yes (notes are the one human-written layer there); the alternative keeps harvested fully read-only.
  5. Mentions in Notes notify nobody today; the Mentioned me view (frame 18) should list notes mentions as well.
  6. Frame 8 (“All discussions in the design”) is approved but unbuilt; does it list Notes edits and range comments next to comments, or only comments?
  7. Outline threshold: 1280 px is the viewport; with the app rail open the document column is then about 880 px. If that feels tight, show the outline at ≥ 1360 px.
