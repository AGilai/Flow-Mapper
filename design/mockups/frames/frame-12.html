Frame 12. Data-store collections as their own lifelines (bug #62)

Maintainer: "a DB collection should be its own lifeline. More than 5 collections fold into one DB lifeline, with the collection name on each arrow. Every DB arrow is labelled with the operation (find-one, ...)." Applies to every flow page that touches a store, harvested or design, with "Data stores" on.

  1. One head per collection (up to 5 per store in this flow): store icon, the collection name as the title, the store name and kind as the sub line (users-db · mongodb). The heads of one store sit together under a grey band with the store's name, the same device the Group-by-subsystem bands use, so "three collections, one database" reads at a glance.
  2. Neutral, as decided in frame 11: no component colour, the stripe stays --t-res grey. Collections never take a slot of the component palette.
  3. Every arrow to a store carries the operation in mono, then the collection when the head does not already say it: find-one users, update-one sessions. Operations are the harvester's words, never rewritten. The step tile and halo behave as on any arrow.
  4. Read vs write: reads keep today's thin grey line with a dashed return; writes are a heavier line (3.2px vs 1.6px) with no return and a shorter bar. The difference is subtle on purpose: the label says which it is, the line weight is the glance cue. Colour never carries it (same token, light and dark).
  5. Order of lifelines: a store's collections appear in the order of the first step that touches them, to the right of the last component, as stores sit today.

12b. More than 5 collections of one store fold into one lifeline

Read: thin line out, dashed return with what came back.Write: heavier line, no return, shorter bar. Both keep the data-store grey (--t-res) and the operation as the label.

When it folds, and what the checkbox does

  • The fold: when this flow touches more than 5 collections of one store, that store becomes one head, "users-db (7 collections)", drawn as a stack. Counted per store and per flow, not per store overall: a store with 40 collections that the flow touches 3 of stays unfolded.
  • Arrow labels then carry both: operation · collection, for example insert-one · audit_events. The dot separates the two; the collection is the part to read.
  • The threshold is a constant (MAX_COLLECTION_LIFELINES = 5), not a setting. The fold is not a click-to-open one like the Squeeze fold: the step panel lists the collections of the selected arrow.
  • "Data stores" keeps its meaning: unchecked it hides every store lifeline and its arrows, folded or not. Nothing else changes.
  • Step walk: the step label is the full operation and collection, insert-one · audit_events, so folded steps stay readable.

Subsystem bands and colour de-clash

  • Two band rows: subsystem bands sit on top of the component heads; store bands sit over the collection heads, same row height. Below Platform, users-svc's collections sit under their store band, so both levels show.
  • Order: components of one subsystem stay together. Components with no groupPath sit under no band.
  • Icons are a built-in set of simple 16px line icons (platform, agent, network, cloud, shield, database), chosen per subsystem in Environment settings › Subsystems: name, icon. A subsystem with no icon shows its name only.
  • Group by subsystem sits next to Data stores and Squeeze: on by default in design envs, off in sealed envs that have no groupPath.
  • Per-flow colour de-clash: in an env with more than 8 components, two components in the same flow can share a palette slot. The later one takes the next free slot for that flow only. The stored env colour is unchanged.
