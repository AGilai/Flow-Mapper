Frame 27. Accounts, ownership and sharing (AWS v1, section 3; awaiting approval)

Sign-in, the user menu, an env picker that groups environments by who owns them, a Share dialog, the read-only view of a published design, the Tokens page and the empty states. Spec: docs/plans/aws-v1.md section 3. Everything is drawn on the real shell (rail, Flows page, 0.51.x); only the marked parts are new.

27a. Sign in, and the user menu

Signed out, the app shows one card: the product line and a single Sign in button that redirects to the Cognito hosted page. Signed in, the rail foot gets a user button (initials, name). It opens a small menu with Tokens and Sign out. An admin sees an Admin chip under the email.

27b. Env picker: grouped by owner, with a visibility badge on every row

The rail’s Environment control opens a list in four groups. Every row has the same badge vocabulary: Private (lock), Shared · N (people and the count of people it is shared with), Published (globe). Harvested environments carry Sealed (shield) and the group note says they are read-only for everyone. “Published” lists other people’s published designs; yours stay under My designs with their Published badge. Below 860px the rail becomes a strip and the picker a sheet (second copy, 760px).

Decisions and notes

  1. Badges. Private, Shared · N and Published are neutral outlined pills; the icon and the word carry the meaning, no status colour (UI-GUIDE section 8). Sealed uses the shield. The same badge appears in the picker, the page head and the dialog.
  2. Colour. Only tokens. Accent is the current rail item, links and the primary button; env dots use the existing --env-N palette.
  3. Hidden means absent. A design you cannot read is not in the picker, search or links: a link to it is “not found”, never “forbidden”.
  4. Roles. Owner: share, publish, delete, apply drafts. Editor: open drafts. Viewer: read. Admin: everything, and the Harvester scope for tokens.
  5. States not drawn. Name autocomplete in the Add field (it lists directory users, same row style as the people list), a network error on Add, and the revoked-token confirmation toast.
