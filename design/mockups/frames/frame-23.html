Frame 23. Store boxes in a flow are clickable (bug #86; maintainer approved the ask, the lead approves the visual)

In an open flow, clicking a Redis or Mongo participant head opens a panel that lists only what this flow touches in that store: per key template or collection the operations, the step numbers (click to jump), the code links, the key's TTL and db index (Redis) or the fields (Mongo), and the keys-registry row when the template has one. A link at the bottom goes to the store page, where the full key list stays. Sample: auth-svc POST /login with p81-production · redis and Account · mongodb. Build spec: docs/plans/store-panel.md.

23a. The panel in an open flow: Redis store selected

POST /login in auth-svc. The p81-production · redis head is pressed (accent outline, tinted fill, lifeline solid). Arrows to other participants dim to 42 %; the five Redis steps keep a filled accent number. The panel takes the same right-hand pane as the step panel and the 0.42.0 “Why incomplete” section, so there is one pane and one close button. Each key template is a card: operations (access chip plus the op name), step chips, code links, then the keys-registry row when there is one (org:{orgId}:policy has none and says so).

23b. The participant head: rest, hover, keyboard focus, pressed

The head is a real control: role="button", tabindex="0", Enter or Space opens the panel, aria-pressed says it is open, a second press or Esc closes. Hover shows a tooltip (Show what this flow touches in this store), which is also the focus-visible hint. The Client and auth-svc heads stay as today: they are not stores and have no panel.

23c. Mongo: a collection and its fields

Account · mongodb is a collection lifeline (one per collection, as drawn today), so its panel is one card for that collection. Field chips are grouped by role (filter, project, update, sort), the same grouping as the step panel. No key template, TTL or registry row for Mongo. If the store folds into one lifeline (more than 5 collections in the flow) the panel opens from that folded head and has one card per collection.

23e. “Key not reported”, and step-chip states

Steps whose details.keyTemplate is missing form one card titled Key not reported (italic, not a mono key), last in the list. It keeps the op rows, step chips and code, and says why when the step carries an unresolved entry (here “computed at runtime”). Right: the step chip in rest, hover, keyboard focus and current (the step last jumped to). Clicking a chip scrolls the diagram to the arrow and flashes it; the panel stays open.

For the lead to confirm (recommendations are in the build spec)

  1. Same pane, not a new surface. The store panel replaces the step panel content (one pane, one close). Esc or the head toggles it closed; clicking an arrow switches back to the step panel.
  2. Step chip = jump and flash, panel stays. The alternative (switch to the step panel) loses the store view on every click.
  3. Delete is derived from the op name. The data has only read, write, read_write; a write whose op name is on a short list (del, unlink, deleteOne, deleteMany, remove, findOneAndDelete, hdel, srem, zrem) shows the delete chip. Everything else follows access.
  4. The panel is in the URL as &store=<store key> so a link restores it. Recommendation: yes, cheap and makes the panel shareable.
  5. Collapse at 8 chips per row; keys sorted by first step; the registry block is always visible, not folded.
