Frame 21. Alternatives and scenarios (change request #82, Additions 4–7; maintainer decisions of 2026-10-22)

For flows where the harvest says “either A or B”. Design only, nothing built. Spec: docs/plans/alternatives.md (last section wins). Example: env attr-fix, safer-auth POST /api/v1/devices/tenant/register, decision “when triggerBy is installation_key or user_reassign → Member.createFromIdp, else → Member.invite”, with a nested “createFromIdp: existing user / new user” that depends on data.

  1. No new row above the flow. The choice is an inline dropdown on the diagram’s either / or divider row; a nested decision gets its own divider and dropdown while its parent arm is visible. The existing header row gets one chip, Scenario (N), opening a popover with every decision (locked ones with their reason) and a reset.
  2. Default = first arm. The flow draws one path and counts are exact. “Both” is an option; only then do ranges (“6–7 steps”) appear: header, step bar. The Flows list always shows the default view’s exact number.
  3. Locked arms (caller’s literal): lock icon, no menu, reason as text with file:line; not in the URL.
  4. URL: ch=<g8>:<arm> lists only choices that differ from the default; detail=full and open=svc likewise.
  5. External is the default everywhere. Internal calls are lazy: a plain “⊕ internal” control per service lifeline (no count) fetches that service’s internal calls; “Full” fetches all. The External | Full switch is small and sits in the header row.
  6. DB grouping: consecutive DB operations fold into “DB: N ops (collections)”, expandable; an external call starts a new group.
  7. Lambdas and callbacks are ordinary internal calls named by file:line; they appear and hide with the other internal calls. No folding, no “on error” markers.
  8. Both in the diagram: divider, a caption per arm and a 3 px gutter marker; no boxes. Step keys never change when you pick an arm.

21a. Default: first arm of every decision, exact counts, External

No new row above the flow. The scenario lives where the decision happens: the either / or divider row in the diagram carries an inline dropdown (accent text, caret) with the “depends on data” badge, and the nested decision gets its own divider with its own dropdown (indented with “└”) while its parent arm is visible. The default is the first arm of each, so the flow draws one path and every count is exact: 6 steps in the header and the step bar. The only addition to the existing header row is a compact Scenario (2) chip (opens 21d) and a small External | Full switch; External is the default everywhere. Each service lifeline other than the database has a plain “⊕ internal” control (no count): it loads that service’s internal calls on demand (21g). URL: nothing, because nothing differs from the defaults.

21d. The Scenario popover: all decisions, a locked arm with its reason, Reset

The chip in the header opens a popover that lists every decision on this flow in one place (useful when the diagram is long, and for the 760 px layout). Here the first decision is locked: the caller passes the literal, so the field is grey with a lock, cannot be opened, and says why in plain text with the file:line link (not a tooltip). The divider in the diagram shows the same lock and a short reason. Locks come from the data and are not in the URL. “Reset to defaults” returns every unlocked decision to its first arm. The chip is accent-coloured while anything differs from the defaults. A flow without decisions has no chip. Several decisions: the list scrolls inside the popover.

21f. Full: every internal call, DB grouping, one group expanded

“Full” (small switch in the header row) loads every service’s internal calls, in the diagram as a dot on the lifeline with a grey “internal” tag. The lifeline controls disappear because everything is already loaded. Counts follow the detail level: 12 steps here against 6 in External. DB expansion is session-only. URL: detail=full only when it differs from the default.

21g. Lazy expand on one service

The control under a lifeline head is a plain “⊕ internal” (no number, because the server does not count what it has not sent). Clicking it fetches that service’s internal calls: first a loading state (the control says “⟳ loading…”, grey skeleton rows appear where the calls will go), then the internal calls of that service only, with the control flipping to “⊖ internal” to hide them again. Other services stay collapsed. The step count follows what is loaded (here 10). URL: open=safer-auth.

For the maintainer to decide

  1. Decision label length. The template name is a whole sentence. Drawn: it trails the dropdown on the divider, clipped, full text on hover. Alternative: the Loader also exports a short label (“triggerBy”).
  2. Step bar with “Both”. Drawn: row numbers on the diagram (1–10, stable keys) and “6–7 steps per path” in the bar. Alternative: restart numbering in each arm.
  3. Hidden-step comment “Show” flips the owning decision to Both. Alternative: only offer the link in the comment, and do not change the diagram.
  4. Locks are not written to the URL (they come from the data). Alternative: write them so a link shows exactly what the sender saw.
  5. Flows without decisions get no chip: the header row is exactly as today apart from the small External | Full switch.
