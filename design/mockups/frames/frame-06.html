Frame 6. Importing what's missing while you work

Even a design that started Blank can pull in components and endpoints from other environments when it needs them. Three ways in: the assistant notices while drafting, a call points at something this environment lacks, or you search.

  1. The assistant checks as it drafts and asks once, with a short list: what matches, what exists elsewhere (import?), what exists nowhere (add as new). It never imports without asking.
  2. A call to something missing shows a neutral notice (not a problem: in a design it's normal) with "Import from prod" when another environment has it.
  3. Import from another environment searches every environment, groups results by environment, and says what comes along. Imports go into the open draft, so they're reviewed and applied like any edit. Their baseline is the source's current revision.
  4. Imported, not new. An imported endpoint is unchanged against its own source, so it gets no spark. The meta line says where it came from ("from prod, imported Oct 22").
