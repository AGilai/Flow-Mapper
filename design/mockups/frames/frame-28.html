Frame 28. Flows page: the diagram first (revision 2: one row, tabs, design and flow descriptions)

Decided after review: the one-row layout (old variant B) is the base; zoom floats on the canvas; the Flows list never auto-collapses; the right pane pushes the diagram. This revision adds what an HLD tool needs and the first version lacked: how several tabs behave, a description of the whole design, and a visible, emphasised description of each flow. With everything collapsed the diagram starts …px from the top of the content area (today: about 310px). With the design card open: …px. With the flow card open: …px. Both open: …px at 1900, …px at 1280.

  1. Collapsed is the default for readers who have seen it. Design card: an "About the design" chip in the row. Flow card: a "! 2" badge on the tab, and the ★ / ! marks stay on the steps in the diagram itself, so the emphasis is visible even when the card is folded.
  2. Expanded cards push the diagram (they are not overlays) and fold back with one click. Each remembers its state: the design card per design, the flow card per flow. First visit to a flow that has notes: open; after you collapse it: collapsed.
  3. Three description levels (28i): design (env), flow, call. The call level costs nothing above the diagram: one line in the caption bar, growing upward over the canvas when expanded (28h).
  4. Never wrapping. The row has four width levels (table in 28c). The active tab is never hidden.
  5. Rejected variant A (two rows plus a design strip, diagram at 96px) is dropped. Its screenshots stay in the scratchpad only.

Purpose

How Catalog, Orders and Search serve the web client, and which subsystem owns which data. A reading aid for the HLD review, not a deployment map.

Scope

In: Shop and Catalog subsystems, their data stores, the client entry points. Out: payments (own design), batch jobs, the admin tools.

Key decisions

Catalog owns product and review data (MongoDB). Orders reads Catalog only through its API, never its DB. Cross-subsystem events go over SNS/SQS, not direct calls.

Constraints

Reads under 200 ms p95. No cross-subsystem DB access. Every public entry point needs auth. One region.

28h. Call description (third level): reader and author

One description per call (step). Reader: the caption bar under the canvas shows the purpose of the current step in one line, with a chevron that grows it into a card (what it does, contract, watch) and a Collapse that folds it back; remembered per flow. Hovering an arrow shows a compact note on the arrow; clicking selects the step and shows the full text under "About this call" at the top of the side pane. Calls with a description carry a small note marker next to the step number; calls flagged Watch also carry the ! badge, and its text links to the flow-level Watch / Risk callout. Author (Edit mode): the card becomes a form with a "Watch this call" switch that creates the linked callout. 1900 and 1280.
