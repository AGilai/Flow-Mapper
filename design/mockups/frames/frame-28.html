Frame 28. Flows page: the diagram first (proposal, two variants)

The maintainer's complaint: the Flows page stacks about 310px of repeated and rarely used controls before the diagram, and the right pane can only be closed. This frame gives the diagram the top of the page. Service and entry id appear once, in the tab. Rare toggles live in one View ▾ popover. The Flows list and the right pane collapse to 28px vertical strips. Diagram starts at …px (A) / …px (B) from the top of the content area at 1900 wide; at 1280 wide …px (A) / …px (B). Today it is about 310px (400px at 1900 including the design strip and pane header).

  1. Variant A, two rows (conservative). Design strip (32px) + tab row (32px) + one toolbar row (32px). Everything visible today stays visible, only the repeats and rare toggles go. Row heights are 32px so targets stay comfortable.
  2. Variant B, one row (aggressive). The design strip folds into a "design" pill on the same row as the tab and toolbar (40px). Share, Baseline and Drafts are in the pill's menu. Zoom floats on the canvas. Biggest diagram, but more is one click away.
  3. Nothing is lost. The mapping table at the end lists where every current control went.
  4. Strips. Both side strips are 28px, text runs top to bottom, with a chevron at the head. Hover shows the full title. State is remembered per user.

28a. Variant A, 1900 × 920, data-store pane open

Design strip, tab row and toolbar take the first 96px. The step caption and legend moved under the diagram. The pane (360px) is unchanged but gets a collapse chevron beside the close ×.
