Frame 8. All discussions in the design (later)

#/discussions?env=sase-hld. Every thread, whatever it's about, in one list. Each row names its subject at its level, and opens the thread where it lives.

  1. The subject column is the anchor: a level chip (Flow, Call, Component, Endpoint, Field, Subsystem link) and the item's name. A thread on a field survives a rename of its message: it's anchored by key, and an anchor that no longer resolves is listed as "subject removed".
  2. Filters: open or resolved, in a draft or in the environment, and by level. The assistant reads the same list through MCP and answers threads it's asked in.
