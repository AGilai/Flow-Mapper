Frame 10. References, importing one level, and partial flows

#/flows?env=sase-hld&draft=hld-import&tabs=sase-agent/in.tunnel.open&step=3. The agent's flow reaches two prod components whose behaviour isn't in the design: users-svc and activitylog. The user is about to import users-svc's posture endpoint so it can be changed.

  1. A reference has a lock. A copied endpoint without its behaviour (handler and call tree not in the design) shows 🔒 on its bar end, on its head and in lists, with the words "reference from prod". It's neutral, not a warning: referencing prod is normal in a design. A component with at least one reference endpoint shows the lock on its head; the panel says which endpoints.
  2. "Import into design" sits next to every lock: at the flow boundary, on the service page row, in the step panel. It never fires directly: it opens the preview on the right.
  3. One level, never recursive. The preview lists what comes along: the endpoint's handler and its call tree inside that component, the calls it makes, the stores it touches, the queues and DTOs it uses. What it calls in other components stays a reference (locked) and is listed under "Stays a reference", each with its own Import, so the next level is always an explicit choice.
  4. Imported is not new. Imported items take the source's current revision as their baseline, so they show no spark; the lock goes and the meta line says "imported from prod, Oct 21". Editing them later marks them Changed.
  5. A partial flow says so. When any step of a flow continues into a reference, a neutral line under the step walk says "Partial flow: 2 steps continue into references", with the names and Import links. The flow list shows a small lock next to such flows.
  6. Editing across a reference warns once. Opening the edit form of a call whose other side is a reference shows a callout in the form: the edit changes the call only, not what happens in the reference. Two buttons: "Import users-svc's flow first" and "Edit the call only". The choice isn't remembered: every such edit asks.
