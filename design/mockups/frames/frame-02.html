Frame 2. System map, component level

#/?env=sase-hld. 14 components in three regions. New ones carry the spark, prod's are quiet, and the two prod components the design changes say how.

  1. Level control: Subsystems · Components (frame 3 is the subsystem level; Domains joins later). At component level the map groups by subsystem instead of team in a design environment. The force layout is held to columns, one per subsystem, in the environment's order.
  2. Region head = name and the split. "Platform: 9 components, 3 new, 6 from prod". No tiles.
  3. New node: the spark at its top right. Shapes keep their meaning (ring service, diamond topic, rounded square client). The agent is a new kind, client, drawn as a device.
  4. Changed prod component: the label says what changed ("+1 endpoint", "+1 call"), so a reviewer sees the design's footprint on prod at a glance.
  5. Edges keep the transport colours, async stays dashed. Edges between two prod components are thin grey: they came along with the copy and aren't part of the design.
  6. Verdict sentence under the title, written from the data, with one callout per real gap (here: a call into the design with no transport chosen yet).
